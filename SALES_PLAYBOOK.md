# SponsorStack sales playbook

## Ideal first buyer

A creator, UGC professional, talent manager, creator educator, or small creator community negotiating recurring brand work.

## Founding-customer offer

**$49 rate review** — one 30-minute session, pricing scenarios for a live brief, and a finished rate card.

Upsell paths:

- $199 personalized calculator and branded rate-card setup.
- $399 white-label edition for a creator community.
- $99 quarterly pricing and licensing review.

## Outreach message

> Most creator calculators ignore usage rights, exclusivity, and production effort—the parts that quietly make a deal expensive. I built a transparent deal-pricing desk that also creates packages and the pitch. I’m doing five founding rate reviews for $49. Want me to run your latest brief through it?

## Five discovery questions

1. What content format performs most consistently?
2. Which past campaigns produced conversions or repeat work?
3. How often do brands request paid usage or raw footage?
4. What is the real production time per deliverable?
5. What deal terms have caused regret?

## Proof to show

Price the same brief with organic-only rights and 12-month paid usage to make the licensing difference visible.
